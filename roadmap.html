ADAPTIVE DEVELOPMENT

A SYSTEM THAT CAN IMPROVE

One of the long-term goals of JAVAS is controlled self-improvement. The objective is not unrestricted modification, but a structured development process where JAVAS can identify weaknesses, propose improvements, test changes and request authorization before deployment.

01
OBSERVE

Identify system behaviour and performance.

02
ANALYZE

Determine potential causes and improvements.

03
PROPOSE

Generate a controlled improvement plan.

04
TEST

Validate the modification in isolation.

05
AUTHORIZE

Require appropriate human permission.
