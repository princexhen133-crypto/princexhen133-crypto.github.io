DEVELOPMENT PRINCIPLES

WHAT GUIDES
THE ROADMAP

HUMAN CONTROL

Powerful capabilities should remain subject to explicit permissions and meaningful human oversight.

MODULARITY

Individual intelligence systems should remain replaceable, testable and independently expandable.

OBSERVABILITY

JAVAS should expose enough system state and activity to make important operations understandable.

ADAPTABILITY

The architecture should support controlled learning, improvement and expansion over time.

PRACTICAL INTELLIGENCE

Intelligence should translate into useful work, analysis, automation and real-world problem solving.

CONTINUOUS DEVELOPMENT

JAVAS is treated as an evolving platform rather than a static application with a fixed endpoint.
