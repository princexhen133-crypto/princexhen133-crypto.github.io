03 / THE VISION

Build an AI that understands context.

The long-term direction of JAVAS is a persistent intelligence layer capable of understanding what is happening, why it matters, what information is missing, and which actions are appropriate.

Instead of treating perception, knowledge, reasoning and action as separate experiences, JAVAS is designed to connect them into a continuous intelligence loop.
