VISION

More than an assistant.

JAVAS is being developed as a personal autonomous intelligence capable of understanding the world around it, maintaining context, conducting research, reasoning through complex problems and interacting with the computer environment.

The long-term objective is an intelligence platform that does not simply wait for commands, but can understand goals, construct plans, request permission when necessary and execute meaningful work.
