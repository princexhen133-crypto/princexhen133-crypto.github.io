SYSTEM OVERVIEW

A layered intelligence architecture.

JAVAS is designed around interconnected layers rather than a single isolated model. Inputs are perceived, transformed into context, processed by cognitive systems and connected to controlled actions.

01 ENVIRONMENT World and systems
02 PERCEPTION Signals and inputs
03 CONTEXT Situation model
04 REASONING Cognitive analysis
05 DECISION Action selection
06 ACTION Controlled execution
